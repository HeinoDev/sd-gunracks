`tools/props/textures/fetch.mjs` downloads them and `tools/props/textures/maps/meta.json` records each one.

## The console

The staff console in `web/` bundles the Inter typeface (SIL Open Font License 1.1, from `@fontsource/inter`) and
icons from Lucide (ISC License, from `lucide-react`). Each package in `web/package.json` carries its own licence text.

## Game content

The weapons a rack holds are the game's own models, drawn by the game at run time; none of them ship with this
resource. The pictures in `images/` and `docs/previews/` show those weapons and a few base-game props, rendered from
Grand Theft Auto V's models. They belong to Rockstar Games and are not covered by this resource's licence.

`bridge/` is shared across Samuel's Development resources.
